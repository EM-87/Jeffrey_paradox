# Three of the phrases Mr. Resetti has the player type as a penance (1156-1171): the GameCube's English
# for two of them has a picture the cartridge's charset lacks, which the draft would print as a question
# mark for the player to type, and for the third a sign the en dial does not have. Ours, from the
# cartridge's (how cool the mole is; no more cheating; no more pressing Reset), 10 characters at most like
# the rest: he compares the 10 bytes typed with the phrase's, spaces after both (ovl_Npc_Majin3,
# func_809B4BB8_jp; the window fills its 10 with spaces, ledit_ovl func_80884B30_jp), so every character
# of a phrase must be one the dial types.
#
# And the 32 words he takes as an insult when one is found anywhere in what was typed (1216-1247). He
# compares a fixed number of bytes of each (func_809B4C18_jp, its table D_809B572C_jp: the first 6 words
# 2 bytes, the next 10 three, then 9 of four, 5 of five, one of 6 and one of 7), whatever the word's length:
# the GameCube's words are longer than their places, so their first letters alone would count. Ours. A
# word as long as its place counts anywhere in what was typed; a shorter one (the first of the threes) is
# compared with the space after it, so it counts at the end of what was typed or before a space. None is
# inside a penance phrase, nor inside a try one slip away from one (a character left out, one too many, a
# wrong one, two swapped): the pairs are capitals or letters no phrase has, since any other pair is one
# slip from some phrase. tools/af_dial.py phrases holds the bank to all of this at every build.
# tools/msgbank.py's dump form; `make script` lays this over the draft.
