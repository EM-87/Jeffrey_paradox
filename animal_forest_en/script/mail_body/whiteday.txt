# Our English for six of the villagers' White Day letters (March 14th, when a present is given back for
# one received on Valentine's Day): the GameCube's English for these numbers says the day is Valentine's
# Day, the GameCube having no White Day, and the cartridge sends them on White Day. From the cartridge's
# text; the header and footer stay the GameCube's. Six lines of the board's 192 pixels at most.
# tools/msgbank.py's dump form; `make script` lays this over the mail_body draft.

## 103

good to me. I thought long
and hard about what to give
you for White Day. Here's to
staying good friends!{cd}

## 104
Hey, I just noticed it's
White Day today! I can't
remember if you gave me
anything on Valentine's Day,
but here's a present anyway,
just in case.{cd}

## 106
So, it's White Day, huh.
This is kind of awkward,
but here's a present.

We should go fishing
together sometime!{cd}

## 107
It's White Day, and I've got
nobody else to send a gift
to. Besides, it's not like
you've never done anything
for me, so... here. Take it.{cd}

## 109
It's White Day today.
For <STR_FREE0>, who's always
looking out for me, with my
thanks.

Here's to more good times!{cd}

## 110

stuff like White Day, but
it's a custom, so I guess
there's no getting out of it.{cd}
