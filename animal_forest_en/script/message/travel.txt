# Our own English for the messages of travel and of starting the game, where the GameCube's text is its
# own console's: a memory card in a slot of the GameCube (and free strings 3 to 5, the slots' names,
# which the cartridge does not fill: their leftovers would show), where the cartridge has the Controller
# Pak, which goes in the controller, and the Game Pak (the cartridge itself). From the cartridge's text:
# the station's master (2383-2403), a villager on travel (2170) and the villager who starts the game,
# one set of messages for each personality (5106-5345: polite, peppy, lazy, jock, cranky, snooty; at
# +10 a house torn down, +14 the town erased, +18/+19 a player remembered or new, +20/+38 the Controller
# Pak pulled out, +22 a player home from travel, +24 a visitor, +30 getting ready, +33 the Pak not
# written, +35 a second traveller home, +36/+37 copying or not). The notices in orange are the same
# words in every set, as in the cartridge's. tools/msgbank.py's dump form, as ours.txt (its header says
# how the build lays it over the bank and what af_text.py holds it to).
